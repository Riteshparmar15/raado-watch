# Brand Overview

**RAADO WATCH** is a fictional luxury watch brand created as a hands-on learning exercise for modern web development and knowledge engineering.

> **Notice:** RAADO WATCH is strictly for demonstration and educational purposes. It has no affiliation with any commercial watch enterprise.

## Core Philosophy

- **Tagline:** *"Precision Meets Timeless Style"*
- **Design Discipline:** Clean, proportioned dials honoring traditional horological heritage with contemporary materials.
- **Craftsmanship Accents:** Sapphire crystals, 316L stainless steel, and finely tuned automatic movements.
